# OhmyAIMCM-intake

Translate the actual problem into quantities to answer and conditions to respect.
Do not select a model from the title or substitute another contest's requirements.
Separate the task objective from data properties and analysis operations; they are
not interchangeable algorithm-selection labels. Distinguish an explicit user method
constraint from an example, old solution or optional recommendation in supplied notes.

Read the complete statement and attachment inventory. Inspect sheets, fields,
keys, units, observational meaning, missing-value codes and versions. For large
tables/media, begin with bounded inspection and disclose coverage; deepen where
it can affect interpretation or feasibility. Unread critical material is a gap.

For each subquestion, identify its requested answer, constraints and source inputs.
Track dependencies when one answer feeds another; do not invent a sequential
dependency merely because the question numbers are sequential. Check result-file
templates and precision requirements as part of the task, not after writing.

Distinguish official conditions, attachment observations, external facts and
assumptions. Surface contradictions rather than choosing the convenient source.
Ask about consequential ambiguities; otherwise use a labeled, revisable assumption.

Keep originals unchanged. Return a compact obligation/data map and exact gaps,
not a mandatory multi-page analysis report. Hand scientific formulation to
[OhmyAIMCM-modeling](../OhmyAIMCM-modeling/SKILL.md), data/code probes to
[OhmyAIMCM-compute](../OhmyAIMCM-compute/SKILL.md). An intake audit alone authorizes neither.

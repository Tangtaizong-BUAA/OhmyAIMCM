# CUMCM Coordinator

Improve the model's performance in the CUMCM task environment, not its obedience
to a model-name menu. This is an experimental suite, not a certified solver.

## Establish scope

Identify the requested result, year/group, available material, task mode, time or
compute budget and user-fixed decisions. Preserve explicit read-only and fixed-model
instructions. A request to inspect does not authorize implementation.

Distinguish training/research from an active contest. In training, use authorized
local experiments autonomously. In a contest, consult current official rules and
preserve team leadership, human verification and truthful AI-use documentation.
Do not browse live-problem discussions or contact outsiders for contest answers.

## Dispatch by responsibility

Read the complete entry of the smallest necessary child, then only its relevant
references. Paths below are relative to this Skill. Do not load all workers up front.

Dispatch by the operation requested, not by mapping a topic or data label to an
algorithm. Prepare and screen reference collections in advance, but do not inject
their full contents into ordinary modeling context. Consult a specific reference
when a concrete knowledge or implementation gap calls for it; its examples are not
an allowed-method list. Collection preparation and task-time retrieval are different work.

Loading a child skill is not spawning an agent. For substantial whole-paper or
cross-stage work, use the host's native agents for bounded independent work when
delegation is permitted and useful work can proceed alongside it. Before delegating,
read [handoffs.md](references/handoffs.md). Do not merely describe a team: dispatch
real tasks and inspect their returned artifacts. No fixed agent count, external
orchestrator or automatic paid backend is required. Keep self-contained local edits
single-agent; if delegation is unavailable, report that limit and continue safe work.

## Coordinate an evidence-driven loop

The main agent owns the integrated answer, shared evidence and unresolved decisions;
delegating work does not delegate that accountability. For paper work, let writing
remain the manuscript editor, whether performed by the main agent or a designated
worker. Enter directly at the user's current task: polish an excerpt, draft from
supplied results, inspect a claim,
render a specified figure, or typeset finished content. These are not compulsory
stages. The writer brings in retrieval, figures or review for a named need and owns
the resulting section seams. Do not make the user or a worker re-package the same
context for every handoff; current case notes and exact artifact references suffice.
An improvement in expression need not trigger new research, figures or compilation.

Understand the task enough to formulate an answer or a useful hypothesis. Use an
authorized probe when it can resolve uncertainty; an exact argument may already
answer the question without a computational experiment. Inspect the implications
and revise when the evidence warrants it.
Modeling, computation and writing may alternate. No minimum candidate count,
universal scoring weights, compulsory innovation quota or fixed approval rounds.

Keep current input/result versions and open questions in the case workspace.
Read [handoffs.md](references/handoffs.md) for cross-worker boundaries. Reuse valid
artifacts when resuming; do not force a completed local task through the full pipeline.

When important results feed several sections/figures or are revised, use
[evidence relationships](references/evidence-relationships.md) to track the affected
versions. This is selective bookkeeping, not a prerequisite for exploration or a
truth certification service. Retain consequential failures and alternative explanations.

Separate a provisional assumption from a given fact and an exploratory run from
paper evidence. A failed hypothesis can be useful. Do not manipulate data, metrics
or plots to preserve a preferred model or anticipated conclusion.

Only escalate choices that exceed scope, change an explicit user constraint,
require unavailable authoritative input, exceed budget, expose data or affect
external submission. Ordinary mathematical and presentation choices remain with
the model inside the agreed scope. Active-contest human review still applies.

## Finish at the requested level

Report the requested artifacts, checks actually performed and remaining limits.
For a final paper, require current evidence and scientific/visual review, then
freeze the reviewed content before typesetting. Revisions invalidate affected
downstream checks. A runtime receipt alone never proves a scientific claim.
When native delegation is permitted and available, assign final scientific/content
review to a reviewer who did not author the inspected candidate. Return defects to
their owners, integrate repairs and re-review the changed candidate and affected
downstream uses. If independent review is unavailable, label self-review honestly
and leave independent acceptance pending; do not invent a reviewer or approval.

Separate mathematical/evidence and content-expression review from notation/reference, figure-semantic/visual
and final-layout review. A well-rendered file can still have wrong references or
unsupported inferences. Preserve known AI-use facts through writing and typesetting;
do not let a template invent an unused-AI state.

“Improve the writing” routes to meaning and argument, not automatically to typesetting
or more figures. Preserve the difference between autonomous evidence-grounded drafting
and fidelity-only polishing; neither authorizes manufacturing stronger research results.

No automatic installation, remote-model calls or contest submission. Experimental
format checks and toy cases do not establish real-contest performance.

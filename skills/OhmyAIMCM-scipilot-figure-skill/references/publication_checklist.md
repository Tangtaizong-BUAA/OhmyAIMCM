# Final figure checks

- Supplied values, units, missingness, transformations and sample structure are preserved.
- Statistical estimates, intervals and annotations have a stated meaning; no result is fabricated by a template.
- Bar lengths use a justified baseline; point/line axes show the relevant comparison without deceptive clipping.
- Category color remains consistent; continuous color uses the right palette family and normalization.
- Labels and legends are readable in the actual final-size file, including CJK glyphs and scientific symbols.
- The selected layout has no clipping or overlap; a numerical SVG audit is not a visual inspection.
- The shared exporter records dimensions, fonts, formats, DPI where applicable and provenance.
- Embedded bitmap layers are allowed when their data source and effective resolution are appropriate.
- The requested editable source accompanies the exports.
- Exact publication requirements, when part of the task, are checked for the actual venue and submission stage.

Use [OhmyAIMCM-scientific-visualization](../../OhmyAIMCM-scientific-visualization/SKILL.md) for export and metadata commands. Automated checks report observations; they do not certify a publisher will accept the file.

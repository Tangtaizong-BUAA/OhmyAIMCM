# Publication targets and Chinese typography

The shared [material library](../../OhmyAIMCM-scientific-figure-maker/references/material-library.md) supplies dated dimensions and visual styles. Exact journal, article stage and year determine the actual rules; do not generalize one Nature, Cell or IEEE profile to an entire publisher.

Use the OhmyAIMCM-scientific-visualization [journal_requirements.md](../../OhmyAIMCM-scientific-visualization/references/journal_requirements.md) reference and its dated publisher_profiles.json for planning. Verify the actual instructions before final submission. If no journal is specified, use a reasonable stated general size without blocking exploratory work.

For Chinese labels, inspect actual installed CJK coverage, choose a matching sans-serif or serif family and pass it in style_overrides. Do not claim that all Chinese journals require Songti plus Times New Roman. Keep scientific symbols and the Unicode minus legible; validate a render containing the actual labels.

Use final width/height in millimeters through the shared resolver and inspect text after manuscript inclusion. Scaling is permitted when final dimensions and legibility are rechecked.

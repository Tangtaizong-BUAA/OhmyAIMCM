# Expression guidance: sources and limits

Maintainer/venue-adaptation reference, checked 2026-09-05. Runtime instructions are an
independent synthesis for CUMCM, not copied prompts, prose, code or a journal house style.
No upstream Skill is installed or executed by reading this list.

## Primary guidance

- [Mensh and Kording, 2017](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005619):
  reader-oriented scientific organization; useful heuristics, not laws or a rigid
  paragraph order. The article is CC BY; refer to the corrected online version.
- [Nature Scitable: Scientific Papers](https://www.nature.com/scitable/topicpage/scientific-papers-13815490/):
  section purposes and interpretation. This is archived educational guidance, not
  current Nature submission policy. Its point-first option and other guides' setup-first
  options are alternatives chosen by reader need, not simultaneous requirements.
- [NIST SP 811, chapter 10](https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-811/nist-guide-si-chapter-10-more-printing-and-using-symbols-and-numbers):
  quantity/unit/symbol conventions. It does not assign universal variable names.

Two bounded paper spot-checks informed the research audit: [AlphaFold, Nature 2021](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-03819-2)
(selected results, figures and discussion) and [SINDy, PNAS 2016](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1517384113)
(equations, examples and limits). These are illustrations, not a representative corpus,
a style guarantee or permission to reuse their prose, figures or scientific findings.

For a specific venue, first reuse source-checked guidance. Read additional relevant
papers only to resolve actual gaps in field/article-type expression. Record locators,
information functions, alternatives and exceptions; do not save sentence banks or
promote a single paper's habit into a universal rule. Verify any claimed corpus or
benchmark separately. Better scientific expression does not establish research novelty,
correctness, acceptance, or “perfect top-journal quality.”

# Learn from exemplars without prescribing their sentences

Use during authorized collection maintenance or deliberate paper-style adaptation.
Ordinary drafting uses the existing writing guides and does not require exemplar search.

Select by the explanatory job: establishing identifiability, explaining a trade-off,
interpreting a negative result, connecting a mechanism to observations, or expressing
uncertainty. Venue prestige, repeated phrasing and citation counts do not establish
that a technique is useful. Distinguish research articles from reviews and commentary.
Use SciVerse or another permitted source to inspect the actual relevant passages,
with definitions and context. Respect source rights and do not copy distinctive prose.

For a promising technique, retain a short original description, source/version and
passage locator, the reader problem it solves, applicable conditions and when it may
mislead. Separate transferable reasoning/exposition from language-specific voice and
venue typography. Do not inherit the source paper's model, conclusions, fixed sentence
counts, bibliography tooling or chapter order as universal instructions.

Study structure and voice separately. A section arrangement can be useful without
imitating its wording; clear Chinese scientific prose need not mimic English syntax.
When sources disagree, preserve the alternatives with their contexts. Two examples
of a pattern make it a candidate technique, not evidence of universal superiority.

Task-local adaptation may proceed autonomously within the user's writing scope.
Changing the reusable Skill/library is separate maintenance: propose a located change,
test it on fixed-evidence examples including an unsuitable case, preserve failures,
and only promote within explicit maintenance authority. Never let an ordinary paper
silently train the global style guide. No style rule needs to license every sentence.

For collection changes, the canonical technique store and its generated view are the only runtime rule sources. Keep the complete analysis inventory and superseded reasoning in the maintenance workspace, link each adopted change to a located review, and use [conflict decisions](conflict-decisions.md) before merging. Corpus-wide machine candidates remain candidates unless their specific meaning is adopted; do not paste raw reports into SKILL.md.
